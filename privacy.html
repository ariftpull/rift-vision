Privacy Policy

Effective 20 September 2026. Rift Vision is made by ariftpull. This policy explains what the app stores, what it sends, and what it never does.

What stays on your device

Your collection, scan history, decks and settings are stored in a database on your phone. Videos you record are saved to your phone’s gallery. None of this is uploaded by the app.

Card recognition runs entirely on your phone. Camera frames are analysed on the device and are not sent anywhere.

What the app fetches

  • Card prices, once a day, from tcgcsv.com (a public mirror of TCGplayer market prices). The request carries no personal information beyond your IP address, which any web request includes.
  • Card images for a few printings that are not bundled with the app, from TCGplayer’s or Riot Games’ image servers, when you view them.

Signing in

Signing in with Google is optional. When you do, the app asks Google for your name, email address and avatar, and stores them on your device so the app can show who is signed in. The app does not read anything else from your account.

The app keeps a Google token in your phone’s secure keystore for one purpose: writing and reading your backup in Google Drive (below). Signing out removes the profile and the token.

Backups

You can back up your openings, trades, decks and settings to a file you keep, or, when signed in with Google, to a private app folder in your own Google Drive that only this app can see (Google’s “application data” folder). Drive backups happen only when you tap Back up or switch on daily backup, and only to your Drive: the developer has no server and never receives your data. You can delete the folder from Google Drive’s settings at any time.

What the app never does

  • No analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No account is required to use any feature.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes its database and settings. Recorded videos remain in your gallery until you delete them.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer through the app store listing.
