Open packs. Know what you pulled.

Rift Vision is a free Android companion for the Riftbound trading card game. Point the camera at each card as you open a pack: it is recognised on your phone, priced, and added to your collection while you record.

Android beta coming soon. Everything runs on your phone; nothing is uploaded.

  • Scan as you open — on-device card recognition, no typing
  • Prices — market prices for every card, refreshed daily
  • Collection — set completion, filters, sorting, value
  • Decks — builder with the official deckbuilding rules, import/export, missing cards and cost
  • Trades — scan what changes hands; your collection follows
  • Export — your opening as a video with prices burned in
  • Backups — to a file, or your own Google Drive
